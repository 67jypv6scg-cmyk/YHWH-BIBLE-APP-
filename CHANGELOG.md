# Noble Vine Study & Discipleship: changes

## 2.0 beta (October 2026)
The second release, for close friends to try.

**New**
- **Favourite verses.** Select verses, tap ♥ Favourite. They are highlighted in your colour (Settings → Favourite verses), and kept in More → My Walk → Favourite verses, where each can have its own colour and themes.
- **Themes.** Tag verse notes, day notes, prophetic words, teaching notes and favourites. Sixteen starter themes, and you can add, rename, recolour or remove your own. More → My Walk → Themes, or search `#theme`.
- **The divine Names, your way.** Settings → How the Name is shown: Hebrew (יהוה), English (Yahowah), both, or with the familiar word [LORD]. The same for the Messiah's name. Changes apply straight away across the whole Bible.
- **Tap a Name to learn what it means**: Hebrew, how it is said, its meaning, and how most English Bibles print it. A Names guide sits in More → Study, and a welcome tip explains it the first time.
- **My music.** Play songs you have saved in Files or iCloud Drive while you read, with a slim player above the tab bar. Settings → Music player switches between My music, Spotify (the earlier dock) and Off.
- **Report a problem**, at the foot of the More menu, lists anything that went wrong so it can be sent to Brad.

**Safer foundations**
- The app is now split into small files, one per feature, instead of one very large file.
- A properly versioned offline copy, with a banner when a new version is ready.
- Backup files are checked before anything is restored; damaged chapters are skipped.
- Screens that load slowly can no longer draw over a screen you have moved on to.
- Backups are now called "Noble Vine backup", and include favourites and themes.

## Before 2.0
Versions 1 to 39 were built step by step in one file: the Restored Names Bible (RNB), Hebrew interlinear for the whole Old Testament, Compare with the WEB and ASV, reading plans and book studies, memory verses, recordings, teaching notes, prophetic words, My Walk, and the Noble Vine name and logo.
